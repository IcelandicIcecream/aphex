A checkbox (`boolean`) field shows its title once, beside the box, instead of also as a label above it, with its description under the box. An object made only of checkboxes (a dish's dietary marks) is a fieldset under its one heading, two to a row where there's room. Each checkbox has its own id, so a list of items no longer repeats one id.

One that folds (`options.collapsible`/`collapsed`) is the same box as a folding fieldset, its heading inside, and folded it says what's ticked: "Vegan, Spicy".
