Three new question kinds: Phone, Date (with "today or later only" for bookings) and Choose one (radio buttons). An answer key no longer has to be typed: left empty, the save makes one from the question (`withFieldKeys`, also at `@aphexcms/plugin-forms/keys` for a site's renderer), and it moves under "More" with the hint text and defaults. Plainer words throughout: Question, Must be answered, Short answer, Dropdown, Tick box, Paragraph, After sending, Thank-you message.
