New plugin: an `icon` field with a searchable picker of Phosphor's 1,512 icons in six weights. The drawing is saved with the choice, so a site draws it with `@aphexcms/plugin-phosphor-icons/icon` and ships no icon set. Saving fills a drawing in from a name alone, and a `search_icons` tool lets the site's AI find names.
